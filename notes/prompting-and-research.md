# Prompting, Research & Source Links

## Start with a Specific Question

A weak topic is: “Write about AI.”

A better question is: “How do frontier AI models affect defensive cybersecurity, and what safeguards are used when models have access to tools or networks?”

Specific questions make research faster and help you avoid vague summaries.

## A Practical Prompt Pattern

Use a prompt that includes a role, topic, audience, constraints, and output format:

```text
Research a recent, credible story about [TOPIC].
Use primary sources or original reporting when possible.
Write a 2–3 paragraph summary for students.
Separate confirmed facts from analysis.
Include direct links to the original sources.
Avoid copying source language word-for-word.
```

For a visual, add the intended use and constraints:

```text
Create a wide editorial hero image for a World News page.
Use a calm navy and teal palette, leave negative space for a heading,
and include no readable text, logos, watermarks, or political endorsements.
```

## Research Rules

1. Prefer a government agency, research organization, company report, or original news organization over an unsourced repost.
2. Check the publication date. Current-events projects need current information.
3. Read the source, not just a search-result preview.
4. Attribute claims: write “The IEA reported…” instead of presenting its conclusion as your own undisputed fact.
5. Link to the exact article or report you used.
6. Do not use AI-generated text as a source. AI can help locate, summarize, and revise information, but the source should be a real publication or primary document.

## How This Project Used Prompts

The workflow moved from broad requests to narrower ones: create editorial images, locate reporting on oil chokepoints, summarize frontier-model security concerns, then revise the article to distinguish containment failures from claims of intentional “escape.” The important part was not the exact wording—it was repeatedly adding scope, audience, evidence requirements, and accuracy checks.
