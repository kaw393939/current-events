# Prompts & Sources

Be specific. Bad prompt: “Write about AI.” Better prompt:

```text
Find a recent, credible story about frontier AI and computer security.
Write a short student-friendly summary.
Link the original source. Clearly say who reported each important fact.
```

For images:

```text
Create a wide editorial hero image for an AI news page.
Use navy, teal, and violet. Leave space for a heading.
No logos, watermarks, or text.
```

Quick rules:

- Read the source, not only the search preview.
- Link the exact article or report.
- Say “The IEA reported…” instead of acting like every fact is your own.
- Ask Codex to revise if the answer is too long, vague, or hard to understand.
